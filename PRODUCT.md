# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers browse and purchase Gritmode streetwear from the storefront. Store administrators manage products, variants, inventory, orders, and operational notifications from the admin dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Gritmode is an e-commerce experience for Vietnamese streetwear. Customers should be able to understand product availability and complete purchases confidently; administrators should be able to manage catalog and inventory states without ambiguity.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, streetwear, practical. The interface should feel direct and confident, with clear state communication and little decorative noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid overly decorative dashboard patterns, playful or ambiguous status treatments, and UI that hides important product or inventory state behind visual effects.

## Design Principles

- Make availability obvious before the customer attempts to buy.
- Preserve the existing monochrome, streetwear-led visual language.
- Keep storefront interactions concise and task-oriented.
- Give administrators explicit controls over catalog presentation and inventory visibility.
- Treat disabled, hidden, selected, and focus states as first-class UI states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-visible focus states. Support `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not communicate availability through color alone; pair muted styling with the text label “Hết hàng”.
